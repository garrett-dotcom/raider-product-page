What is RAIDER?

Mac software in development, focused on storage awareness, data protection, and thermal awareness. It’s being built for people whose work depends on their drives.

Who is it for?

People creating, editing, and keeping important work on a Mac. If your drives hold projects, media, or archives you care about, you’re who we’re building for.

Can I download it now?

Not yet. RAIDER is still in development. There is no public download or announced release date. Get in touch if you’d like to express early interest.

What will you share next?

Product previews, compatibility, and availability when they’re ready. For now, development continues behind the scenes.
