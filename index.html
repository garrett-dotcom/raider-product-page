Storage & Thermal

A clearer view of your storage.

RAIDER brings storage organization, device health, and thermal controller telemetry into a macOS application.

Storage management

Tools for inspecting connected storage, organizing files, and managing copy and comparison workflows.

Drive health

Work in progress on read-only drive identity and SMART health reporting through supported USB storage bridges.

Thermal telemetry

Controller telemetry over serial and authenticated local-network connections, with sensor freshness and commissioning status.

Currently in development

RAIDER is being developed for macOS, with Mac App Store distribution planned. There is no public download or release date yet.

Native drive-health access and physical controller integration are undergoing development and testing. Device compatibility and hardware operation have not yet been fully verified.
