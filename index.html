04 / A FEW CLEAR ANSWERS

MORE TO COME
What is RAIDER?

A Mac application in development that brings storage visibility, data protection, and thermal awareness into one experience. The focus is practical: understand your drives, follow protected copies, and see more of the environment they work in.

Is this software or a hardware product?

The application is the focus. Cooling control is designed to work with compatible controller hardware. Supported hardware and system requirements will be published as the product gets closer to release.

Can I download it now?

Not yet. RAIDER is still in development. There is no public download or announced release date. Get in touch if you’d like to express early interest.

What will you share next?

More about the product experience, compatibility, and availability as those details are ready. We’re keeping unreleased interface and implementation details private while development continues.
